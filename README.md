# IQM Quantum Summer School 2026

A free, three-day online hands-on program introducing quantum computing—from core concepts and algorithms to pulse-level execution on superconducting quantum hardware.

## Overview
- **Dates:** August 25–27, 2026
- **Format:** Online, 3 days (4 hours of lectures and exercises daily; two sessions per day across timezones)
- **Cost:** Free
- **Audience:** Beginners, students, and quantum computing enthusiasts (no prior quantum background required; basic Python helps)
- **Certificate:** Available upon completion

## What You'll Cover
- **Foundations:** Classical bits vs. qubits, quantum gates, and circuit basics
- **Algorithms:** Bernstein–Vazirani, Grover's search, and QAOA for optimization
- **Hardware & Control:** Superconducting quantum processors, qubit mapping and routing, pulse-level control, and noise management

## Tools & Platforms
- **IQM Resonance:** Cloud access to run circuits and experiments on real quantum hardware
- **Qrisp:** Quantum software and algorithm development
- **IQM Pulla:** Pulse-level access to superconducting hardware
